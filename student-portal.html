Internship Policy

Rules, guidelines, and academic requirements for internships

Placement Policy 2025-26

Official policy document from the Training & Placement Office

download Download PDF
  • Students must have a minimum CGPA of 6.0 (no active backlogs) to be eligible for campus placements.
  • All mandatory internship hours must be completed before final placement registration.
  • Students must maintain a minimum 75% attendance as per university norms.
  • Final year (BE) students are eligible for full-time placement drives. TE students may apply for internship drives only.
  • Mandatory internship duration: minimum 8 weeks for TE students, 16 weeks for BE students.
  • Internships should preferably be undertaken during semester breaks or as part of the Industrial Training curriculum.
  • Students must register their internship with the TPO cell within the first week of commencement.
  • Weekly progress reports must be submitted to the departmental coordinator.
  • A student can accept a maximum of 2 job offers through campus placement. After 2 offers, the student is out of the placement process.
  • Students must attend all pre-placement talks they have registered for. No-shows will face a one-drive suspension.
  • Once an offer is accepted, it cannot be revoked by the student. Reneging on an accepted offer will result in deregistration from the placement cell.
  • Professional attire is mandatory during all placement-related events.
  • Any form of malpractice during tests/interviews will lead to permanent debarment.
  • Students must submit the following documents at the start of placement season: updated resume, academic transcripts, ID proof, and passport-sized photograph.
  • Internship completion certificate must be submitted within 2 weeks of internship end date.
  • A detailed internship project report must be submitted to the department for evaluation.
  • All documents must be uploaded to the student portal. Physical copies may be requested.
